1. Introduction

Potencia Global Hub ("we", "our" or "us") develops and publishes mobile applications and digital services. We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information is collected, used, stored and shared when you use our applications.

This Privacy Policy applies to the applications specifically identified as covered by this policy. Each application may have additional information describing its individual features and data practices.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the application and the features you use, we or our service providers may process the following categories of information:

  • Device information: device model, operating system, app version, language and technical identifiers.
  • Usage information: app interactions, feature usage, session information and diagnostic events.
  • Crash and performance information: error reports, crash logs and technical performance data.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers, ad interactions and information used to deliver or measure advertisements.
  • Purchase information: subscription status, product identifiers, purchase history and entitlement information.
  • User-provided information: information you voluntarily provide when using an app or contacting support.
  • Permission-based information: information accessed through device permissions, such as photos, videos, camera, files or location, where a particular app requires those permissions.

Not every application collects every category listed above. The information processed depends on the features and services of the individual app.

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining application features.
  • Improving functionality and user experience.
  • Monitoring performance and diagnosing technical problems.
  • Managing subscriptions, purchases and premium entitlements.
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements where applicable.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorised activity.
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements.

Some applications require permissions to access device features or files. For example, an application may request access to photos, videos, camera, storage or location to provide a feature you choose to use.

Permissions are used for the purposes explained by the relevant application. You can manage or revoke permissions through your device settings. Some features may become unavailable if you deny or revoke a permission.

Whether information stays on your device or is transmitted to a service provider depends on the specific application's implementation. Please consult the app-specific information for details.
